Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This covers the Enginie website and the Enginie Android app, which shows the same site.

The short version

Enginie doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

The microphone and your recordings

Enginie asks for the microphone only when you choose to record a noise. The recording is analyzed on your own device. It is never uploaded, and nobody else can hear it.

If you save a recording, it’s kept in your browser’s storage on that device. It stays there until you delete it in Enginie, clear the site’s data or uninstall the app.

Your answers and settings

Your answers to the questions and choices such as Mechanic mode are kept on your device only.

What the host sees

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical details such as your IP address when your device loads the pages. Enginie doesn’t get or use that information. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Enginie isn’t aimed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/dhyan-gesus/Enginie.
